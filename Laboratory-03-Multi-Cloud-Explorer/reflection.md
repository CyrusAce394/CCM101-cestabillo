# Mission Reflection

Reviewing the multi-cloud market gives important understanding of how the contemporary IT infrastructure looks like. Among other major cloud providers reviewed, Google Cloud Platform seems to be the one with the highest levels of technical advancement in terms of containerization, data analysis, and artificial intelligence services.  the developer of Kubernetes and TensorFlow platforms, Google Cloud has its own high-level offerings such as Google Kubernetes Engine and BigQuery.

Is i learn the differences in technical aspects of services provided, AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud have a lot of structural similarities. First of all, all these platforms work on the same principle of elasticity and pay-as-you-go model of service delivery based on vast data center network presence. Secondly, all three platforms offer functional equivalents in terms of key components of any computer architecture, namely virtual machine instances, scalable object storage, virtual network, and identity management. I learned that, all platforms have comprehensive web-based management interfaces and administrative APIs.

TO decide on which cloud provider to use is more often dictated by particular requirements of business operations rather than by the amount of offered features. Issues such as compatibility with existing software ecosystem (for example, Microsoft Active Directory installations), compliance issues, total budget, and staff competence can define the choice of cloud provider. The experience as a Cloud Solutions Consultant underlined that a good architect should weigh technical capabilities and business needs equally and choose the cloud platform that will help his/her client to solve his/her problem.

Finishing the mission I greatly improved the quality of my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio in terms of structuring research materials, developing a framework for making recommendations based on scenarios, creating equivalence of services map, and analyzing Linux environment migration..
